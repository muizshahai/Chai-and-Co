The menu

Every chai can be made with oat or almond milk for $0.75. Snacks and sweets are baked fresh each morning, and the samosas and bun kebabs come from a family kitchen in Richardson.

About the extra feature

Build Your Chai lets you design your own drink by choosing a base, milk, spice and sweetness. The cup changes color, steam and toppings as you build it, then names your drink and prices it so you can save the order.
